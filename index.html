Data Center Decision Overview

Project: Norwegian Municipality Infrastructure Plan

1. Summary & Situation

The current facility is at a turning point. It still works, but it's becoming outdated and less efficient, and it doesn't give as much value locally anymore.

  • Current Efficiency: Low (loses a lot of energy as heat)
  • Workforce Trend: -15% per year because of more automation and remote work
  • Maintenance: Increasing about 8% every year

2. Options to Consider (Click to Expand)

Click each option to see details and expected effects.

Option A: Upgrade the Existing Facility

Main goal: Reuse the building and make it more energy-friendly by using leftover heat.

Metric What to Expect
Cost Medium (uses what already exists)
Energy Savings About 35% lower power use
Local Benefit Can heat around 500 homes nearby

Option B: Build New Facility

Main goal: Maximum performance and very high reliability.

Metric What to Expect
Cost High (new land and building needed)
Environmental Impact High at start (materials like concrete and steel)
Lifespan 25+ years

Option C: Shut Down the Facility

Main goal: Remove risk and free up the land for new use.

Metric What to Expect
Cost High (removal and cleanup needed)
Jobs Loss of about 20 technical jobs
Opportunity Land can be used for housing or parks
