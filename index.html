Strategic DataCenter Decision Matrix

Project: Norwegian Municipality Infrastructure Lifecycle

Status: Critical Assessment Phase (FY2026)

1. Executive Summary & Context

The existing facility is currently at a technological crossroads. While functional, it represents a "Legacy Liability" due to rising PUE (Power Usage Effectiveness) and diminishing local employment returns.

  • Current Efficiency: Poor (High waste-heat leakage)
  • Workforce Trend: -15% YoY due to remote automation
  • Maintenance: Escalating 8% annually

2. Strategic Options (Expand for Deep-Dive)

Option A: Modernize & Retrofit (Recommended)

Primary Goal: Circular Economy integration via waste-heat recovery.

Metric Projection
Capital Expenditure Moderate (Utilizes existing shell)
Energy Savings 35% Reduction in Grid Demand
Local Benefit Heating for ~500 nearby homes

Option B: Greenfield Build (Next-Gen)

Primary Goal: Absolute efficiency and Tier IV reliability.

Metric Projection
Capital Expenditure High (New land/infrastructure)
Environmental Cost High Initial Carbon (Concrete/Steel)
Operational Life 25+ Years

Option C: Strategic Decommission

Primary Goal: Risk elimination and urban repurposing.

Metric Projection
Exit Cost High (Equipment recycling/Site remediation)
Job Impact Direct loss of 20 Technical Roles
Opportunity Land available for Housing/Parks
